Famous Cities

Explore Top Cities

Cairo

egypt’s capital and its cultural and historical heartbeat. It seamlessly blends Islamic, Coptic, and modern history, featuring iconic landmarks such as Khan El-Khalili, the Mosque of Muhammad Ali, and Cairo Tower.

Alexandrian

The "Bride of the Mediterranean" and Egypt’s second capital. Its rich Alexandrian history blends Greco-Roman culture with coastal charm, home to the Library of Alexandria and the Citadel of Qaitbay.

Luxor

The archaeological capital of the world, housing nearly a third of the world’s ancient monuments. It is famous for its grand temples, including Karnak and Luxor Temples, along with the rich heritage of the East and West Banks

El Gouna

A modern resort town featuring unique architecture built around picturesque lagoons. It is world-renowned for its stunning beaches, water sports, and hosting the prestigious El Gouna Film Festival.
